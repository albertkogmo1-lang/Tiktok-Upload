TikTok Clip Uploader

A personal tool for uploading video clips to TikTok

TikTok Clip Uploader is a personal Chrome browser extension that lets an individual creator connect their own TikTok account and upload a locally selected video clip to their profile, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

What it does:

  • Authorizes the user's TikTok account via OAuth (Login Kit)
  • Lets the user select a video file from their own device
  • Uploads the video to the user's own TikTok account via the Content Posting API, either as a private draft or a direct post

No content is accessed from or posted to any account other than the one that explicitly authorizes the extension. No data is shared with third parties.
