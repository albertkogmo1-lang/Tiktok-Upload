TikTok Clip Uploader

Upload your own clips to your own TikTok account — built by a creator, for creators

TikTok Clip Uploader is a free Chrome browser extension that lets any TikTok creator connect their own account and upload a locally selected video clip, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

How it works:

  • You connect your own TikTok account via TikTok's official OAuth login screen
  • You select a video file from your own device
  • The extension uploads it to your own TikTok account — either as a private draft to finish posting yourself, or as a direct post

Your data stays yours: your TikTok access token is stored only in your own browser. Our backend never stores your login credentials, your videos, or any of your TikTok data — it only relays your upload request to TikTok's API and forgets it immediately after.

This is a free, independent tool built for the creator community. It is not affiliated with TikTok Inc., and it does not manage, access, or post to any account other than the one you personally authorize.
