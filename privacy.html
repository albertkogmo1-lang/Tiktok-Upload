Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tiktok Upload ("the App") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the App via TikTok Login Kit, the App may access:

  • Basic TikTok profile information (e.g., display name, avatar)
  • An access token used to post video content to your TikTok account on your behalf

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed is used solely to authenticate you and to upload video content to your own TikTok account. The App does not sell, share, or use this information for advertising or any purpose beyond its core function.

3. Data Storage

Access tokens are stored only as needed to perform uploads and are not shared with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The App interacts with TikTok's API. Use of TikTok is subject to TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You may revoke the App's access at any time via your TikTok account settings, which will disable further use of your access token. Contact the developer to request deletion of any locally stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required to hold a TikTok account.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
