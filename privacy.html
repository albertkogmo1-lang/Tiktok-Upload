Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTok Clip Uploader ("the App") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the App via TikTok Login Kit, the App accesses:

  • Basic TikTok profile information (e.g., display name, avatar)
  • An access token used to upload video content to your own TikTok account on your behalf

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed is used solely to authenticate you and to upload the video content you select to your own TikTok account. The App does not sell, share, or use this information for advertising or any purpose beyond this core function.

3. Data Storage

Your TikTok access token is stored only locally, in your own browser's extension storage. It is never transmitted to or stored on our backend service. Our backend acts only as a stateless relay: it forwards your upload request to TikTok's API using the token your browser sends along with the request, and it does not log, save, or retain your token, your video content, or any other account data after the request completes.

4. Third-Party Services

The App interacts directly with TikTok's API. Use of TikTok is subject to TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Since your access token is stored only in your own browser and never retained by our backend, you can remove all App-related data at any time by revoking the App's access in your TikTok account settings and/or removing the browser extension. There is no server-side account or data for us to delete on your behalf, because none is stored.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required to hold a TikTok account.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
